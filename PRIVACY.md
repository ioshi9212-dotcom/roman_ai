# RomanAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

RomanAI is a custom interactive-fiction service that uses a backend API to maintain persistent novel sessions.

## Data processed
RomanAI may process and store content submitted through the service, including novel setup information, fictional character data, story turns, session state, chronology, relationship state, and character memory. Technical request metadata may also be processed by the hosting infrastructure.

## Purpose
Data is used only to provide the requested RomanAI functionality, including maintaining continuity between turns and chats, recovering session state, and operating the service.

## Storage and service providers
RomanAI data may be stored or processed by infrastructure providers used to operate the service, including Railway, GitHub, and OpenAI/ChatGPT when the user interacts with RomanAI through ChatGPT. Their own privacy terms may also apply to their services.

## Sharing and sale of data
RomanAI does not sell user data and does not use session content for advertising. Data is not intentionally shared with third parties except as required to operate the service, comply with law, protect the service, or respond to valid legal requests.

## Retention
Persistent novel-session data may remain stored for as long as needed to provide session continuity or until it is removed from the service's persistent storage. Backups or infrastructure-level copies may remain for a limited period according to the relevant hosting provider's practices.

## Security
Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored service data, but no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## User responsibility
Users should not submit passwords, authentication secrets, payment-card information, government identifiers, or other information that is unnecessary for the fictional-writing service.

## Changes
This policy may be updated when the service or its infrastructure changes. The current version will be published at this page.
